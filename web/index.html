Repo Design API docs

assent is the human approval and escalation layer a durable-workflow engine leaves out. A request moves through a chain of ordered steps; each step gates on a rule — anyone, a quorum of M, or unanimous — over its approvers. Build one below and drive it click by click: approve and reject, delegate while an approver is away, hit the four-eyes wall, and tick an SLA clock until it escalates.

One page, three libraries. assent owns the approval logic and composes three first-party siblings, so it carries no third-party dependencies. Everything below runs in your tab over the pure library compiled to WebAssembly — no backend, no network. Reload to reset. Built from zkrebbekx/assent.

fsmx drives the lifecycle machine — Draft → Pending → Approved / Rejected / Withdrawn. An illegal transition is impossible.
sundial runs each step's SLA over a business calendar. On breach, assent applies the escalation action once.
chronicle holds the append-only audit trail — actor, authority, reason, timestamp. No entry is ever rewritten.

Four-eyes & SoD

The maker cannot be a checker. Four-eyes bars the physical person — not just the counted authority — so borrowing an approver's delegation does not get the maker in.

Delegation provenance on the trail

An approver on vacation delegates to a deputy. A decision the deputy makes counts once, under the authority, and the trail records both — “carol, as bob”. Outside the window it is ErrNoDelegation.

SLA escalation clock = sundial

Each step may carry an SLA over a business calendar (Mon–Fri, 09:00–17:00). assent starts a sundial timer at step entry and polls it on Tick. On breach it applies the action once — here, add a fallback approver.

The audit trail store = chronicle

Every event is an append-only entry on a chronicle log — the submit, each decision (with actor, authority and reason), each advance, warn, escalation and state change. It is never rewritten.
